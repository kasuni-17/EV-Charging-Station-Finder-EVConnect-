Our Mission

Accelerating Sri Lanka's Transition to Sustainable Transportation

At EvConnect, we believe that the future of transportation is electric. Our mission is to make EV charging accessible, reliable, and convenient for everyone across Sri Lanka.

We're building a comprehensive network of charging stations powered by 100% renewable energy, ensuring that every charge contributes to a greener future for our island nation.

Since our founding in 2023, we've grown from a single station to a nationwide network, serving thousands of EV owners and helping reduce carbon emissions across the country.
